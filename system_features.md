# Enterprise Cloud ERP - System Features

The Enterprise Cloud ERP system is a comprehensive, multi-tenant application designed to handle all aspects of enterprise operations. Below is a detailed breakdown of all the modules and features available in the system.

## 1. Multi-Tenancy & Administration
- **Company Management:** Support for multiple tenants (companies) in a single database.
- **Subscription Plans:** Dynamic allocation of module access based on subscription tiers (e.g., Core, Plus, Enterprise).
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Customizable roles with fine-grained permissions and scopes across the system.
- **Audit Logging:** System-wide action tracking (who, what, when) for security and compliance.
- **API Keys Management:** Ability to generate and revoke API keys for external integrations.
- **Approval Policies & Workflows:** Configurable multi-step approval rules for sensitive operations (e.g., exits, profile changes).

## 2. Human Resources (HR) & Payroll
- **Employee Directory & Profiles:** Track employee details, branches, and custom fields. Self-service profile updates (subject to HR approval).
- **Attendance Tracking:** Real-time clock-in/out, GPS/IP verification, and configurable attendance policies.
- **Leave Management:** Request, approve, and track employee leave balances.
- **Performance Management:** Track Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) and employee goals.
- **Onboarding & Offboarding:** Structured workflows for new hires and employee exits (Exit Requests).
- **Payroll Processing:** Dynamic salary bands, payroll groups, and comprehensive payroll tax configurations. Generate automated payslips.

## 3. Financial Accounting & Budgeting
- **General Ledger (GL):** Chart of accounts to track all financial activity.
- **Journal Entries & Fiscal Periods:** Double-entry accounting system with automated balancing and fiscal period closures.
- **Accounts Payable (AP) & Receivable (AR):** Manage vendor bills, customer invoices, and their associated payments.
- **Banking & Reconciliation:** Bank account management, transaction logging, and automated statement reconciliation.
- **Fixed Asset Management:** Track assets, run automated depreciation schedules, and record disposals.
- **Budgeting & Cost Centers:** Define financial budgets and track departmental/project costs against them.
- **Taxation & Compliance:** Manage tax codes, auto-calculate VAT/sales tax, and handle electronic tax return filings.
- **Multi-Currency:** Dynamic currency conversion rates and multi-currency transactions.

## 4. Sales & CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
- **Lead Pipeline:** Track leads from prospect to close. Assign ownership and track stages.
- **Sales Orders & Quotations:** Generate quotes and convert them directly to sales orders.
- **Activity & Email Logs:** Keep a comprehensive log of interactions (tasks, calls, emails) tied to individual clients or leads.
- **Sales Targets:** Define and monitor KPI targets for the sales team.

## 5. Point of Sale (POS)
- **Retail Management:** Complete POS interface for cashiers.
- **Products & Categories:** POS-specific product catalogs.
- **Shift Management:** Track cash drawer openings, closings, and discrepancies.
- **Sales & Returns:** Process transactions, apply discounts, generate receipts, and manage customer returns.
- **Daily Reporting:** Generate automated end-of-day sales reports (Z-reports).

## 6. Inventory & Manufacturing
- **Inventory Tracking:** Real-time stock levels, multi-warehouse support, and inventory adjustments.
- **Bill of Materials (BOM):** Define material requirements and cost analyses for manufactured products.
- **Work Orders:** Schedule and track manufacturing runs.
- **Quality Control:** Enforce quality checks and inspections on manufactured batches.
- **Maintenance Tasks:** Schedule preventative or corrective maintenance for machinery and physical assets.

## 7. Communication & Collaboration
- **Team Chat:** Department-specific chat rooms restricted to active team members.
- **Custom Groups:** Create ad-hoc chat groups for cross-functional collaboration.
- **Direct Messaging:** 1-on-1 private messaging between employees.
- **Company Announcements:** Broadcast company-wide news or alerts with pinning functionality.
- **Polling System:** Interactive company-wide or department-specific polls and voting.
- **Knowledge Base & LMS:** Create internal Wiki articles and host Learning Management System courses for staff training.

## 8. Procurement
- **Vendors:** Track supplier details and performance.
- **Purchase Orders (POs):** Request and approve procurement of goods.
- **Request for Quotation (RFQs):** Solicit competitive bids from multiple suppliers.

## 9. Helpdesk & Support
- **Ticketing System:** Internal support requests with priority leveling and status tracking.
- **Ticket Messaging:** Collaborative internal messaging thread on support tickets.
- **Attachments:** Support for uploading debugging info or issue screenshots.

## 10. Advanced Enterprise & Compliance
- **Intercompany Transactions:** Seamless automated tracking for transactions between sister entities.
- **Consolidation Rules:** Aggregate financial reports across multiple related tenants.
- **Document Management:** Centralized secure vault for storing sensitive managed documents with visibility scopes.
- **Compliance & Filing Deadlines:** Centralized dashboard for managing regulatory compliance checks and government filing deadlines.
